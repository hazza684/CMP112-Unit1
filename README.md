Game concept:
Lore: Bob the construction worker misplaced his lunch on the job and needs to find the pieces before lunch time ends. He runs around a construction site built on a tower collecting ingredients from his "friends" 
and then has to make it to his eating spot. 

Gameplay:
* Climbing up tower (Donkey kong style)
* Talking to co-workers
* 5 minutes
* get ingredients around map to assemble the sandwich.
* The towers and environment are all built using unity assets.
* Interactable/objects are all drawn using pixel art.

Genre - Platformer
Design - 3d engine 2d gameplay.
Objects - Towers, sandwich, player(bob the construction worker), other construction workers, ladders, utilities (fridges, microwaves).
Player physics:
* Gravity (dropping down)
* jumping
* Ice/grease physics
* Keyboard movements
Goal - Make the sandwich, get to the top of the tower. 


Background/art:
* Tall towers/skyscrapers in backround
* Gurders surround playable tower.
